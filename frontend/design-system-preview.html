EvacWay Shared Design System

Token-driven, mobile-accessible, unified design system for EvacWay.

v1.0.0 Active

Core Design System Rules

  1. One primary button per view.
  2. Color carries meaning only. Red means danger ONLY; amber means caution ONLY; never use them for decoration.
  3. Nothing overlaps another control.
  4. Tap targets at least 44 px.
  5. Body text at least 14 px (12 px only for captions).
  6. Every list has loading, empty and error states.
  7. Destructive actions need a confirmation.
  8. No horizontal scrolling at 320 px.
